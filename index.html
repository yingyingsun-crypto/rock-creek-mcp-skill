KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Useful, not generic.

A / ARRIVE

Start at Glover Road

Parking and trail access are available at 5200 Glover Road NW, Washington, DC 20015.

Directions ↗
B / TRANSIT

Use Metro + bus

Tenleytown-AU and Fort Totten are the closest Metro stations to the Nature Center area. From Tenleytown-AU, connect to the C81 bus.

Check WMATA ↗
C / WEATHER

Expect a real season

DC summers are hot and humid. Spring and fall are milder; low areas near the creek can have reduced cellular coverage.
